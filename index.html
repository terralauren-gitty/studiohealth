Coverage & Billing

We work with your insurance

Most treatments at StudioHealth are covered — at least in part — by extended health, ICBC, WCB, or MSP. We handle the paperwork so you can focus on getting better.

Book & Use Your Coverage
🚗
ICBC Motor Vehicle Claims
Injured in a car accident? Our chiropractors and RMTs are registered ICBC providers. Initial exam is just $29, follow-up treatments have no co-pay. We handle your claim directly.
🏢
Extended Health Plans
We direct bill most major extended health providers. Chiropractic, RMT, and acupuncture are commonly covered. Ask us to check your plan at booking.
🦺
WorkSafeBC (WCB)
Workplace injuries can be treated and billed directly to WorkSafeBC. Contact our office immediately after a workplace injury to get coverage started.
🏥
BC MSP (Medical Services Plan)
BC residents on premium assistance or adults over 65 may qualify for partial chiropractic coverage under MSP. Ask our team about eligibility.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you accept new patients?
Yes! StudioHealth is currently accepting new patients for all services — chiropractic, RMT, and acupuncture. You can book online through our JaneApp booking system or call the clinic directly.
What should I bring to my first visit?
Please bring your extended health insurance card, a valid government ID, and any relevant medical imaging (X-rays, MRI results) if available. Arrive 10 minutes early to complete your intake paperwork.
Do you offer direct billing for insurance?
Yes — we direct bill most major extended health plans, removing the need to pay upfront and submit claims yourself. Contact us ahead of your appointment to confirm your plan is covered.
How does ICBC treatment work at your clinic?
If you've been in a motor vehicle accident, we can begin treating you under ICBC immediately. Your initial chiropractic exam is $29 and follow-up treatments have no co-pay. We handle the claim directly with ICBC on your behalf.
Is chiropractic care safe during pregnancy?
Yes, with a trained practitioner. Our chiropractors use modified techniques for pregnant patients, and we also offer specialized prenatal massage with positioning designed for all trimesters of pregnancy.
Where is the clinic and is there parking?
We're at 4196 Main Street, Vancouver (corner of Main & 26th Ave). Street parking is available on Main Street and nearby side streets. We're also accessible by the #3 Main bus and a short walk from King Edward Canada Line station.

Ready to start feeling better?

New patients are always welcome. Book online in under 2 minutes, or call us directly and we'll find the right time and practitioner for you.
