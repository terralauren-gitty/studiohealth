FAQ

Common questions

Do you accept new patients?
Yes! StudioHealth is currently accepting new patients for all services — chiropractic, RMT, and acupuncture. Book online through JaneApp or call us directly.
What should I bring to my first visit?
Please bring your extended health insurance card, valid government ID, and any relevant medical imaging (X-rays, MRI) if available. Arrive 10 minutes early to complete your intake form.
Do you offer direct billing for insurance?
Yes — we direct bill most major extended health plans. No need to pay upfront and submit claims yourself. Contact us before your appointment to confirm your plan is covered.
How does ICBC treatment work at your clinic?
After a motor vehicle accident, we can start treatment immediately under ICBC. Your initial chiropractic exam is $29 and follow-up treatments have no co-pay. We handle the claim directly with ICBC.
Is chiropractic care safe during pregnancy?
Yes, with a trained practitioner. Our chiropractors use modified techniques for pregnant patients. We also offer specialized prenatal massage with positioning designed for all trimesters.
Where is the clinic and is there parking?
We're at 4196 Main Street, Vancouver (corner of Main & 26th Ave). Street parking is available nearby. Accessible by the #3 Main bus and a short walk from King Edward Canada Line station.

Ready to start feeling better?

New patients are always welcome. Book online in under 2 minutes, or call us directly and we'll find the right time and practitioner for you.
